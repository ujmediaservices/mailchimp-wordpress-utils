## A Shinto shrine you've never seen

Walk the trails of the Ojiragawa Gorge in Hokuto, Yamanashi Prefecture, and you'll find a site many visitors to Japan never encounter. The Kaikomagatake Shrine enshrines the god Ōkuninushi-no-Mikoto. Surrounded by towering cedars beside a flowing river, the shrine is said to bestow blessings for good health, business prosperity, and safe childbirth.
